Cookie Policy

Effective Date: March 3, 2026. How rayBEN uses cookies and similar technologies.

Transparency & Consent • Last Updated: March 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently and provide analytical or advertising insights to site operators.

2. How rayBEN Uses Cookies

rayBEN does NOT use cookies for its core photo conversion functionality. All image re-encoding, aspect ratio calculations, and EXIF processing happen strictly in local browser volatile memory (RAM). We do not save cookies identifying your uploaded images.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We work with trusted third-party partners who may set cookies when you browse our site:

  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): Help us understand how visitors engage with the site (e.g., conversion completion rates, bounce rates, and device categories) so we can fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Help deliver relevant advertisements and prevent the same ad from showing repeatedly. Google uses cookies like the __gads cookie to serve ads based on prior visits.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. For more information:

  • Opt out of personalized Google ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics tracking across the web via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
