3:4 Aspect Ratio Workflow

About Meta Spin Studio

Smart wearables and modern phone cameras capture moments in varying orientations and aspect ratios. Meta Spin Studio makes formatting, framing, and sharing to Instagram Stories effortless and private.

Why 3:4 Portrait?

3:4 portrait (3024 × 4032) is the native sensor ratio of smart wearable camera glasses and the optimal format for mobile portrait viewports and Instagram Story feeds without awkward letterboxing or unwanted auto-cropping.

Static Vanilla Architecture

This entire web application is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. All pages reside in the main directory. There are zero backend servers, zero build steps required for deployment, and full compatibility with GitHub Pages, Netlify, or any static host.
