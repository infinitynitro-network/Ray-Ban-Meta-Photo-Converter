How to Use Ray-Ban Meta Photos for Instagram Story 3D Spin

Everything you need to know about triggering Instagram Story's interactive 3D parallax spin view effect with converted or authentic smart-glasses photos.

By rayBEN Research Team • 5 min read • Updated March 2026

What is the Instagram 3D Parallax Spin Effect?

When you upload a photo taken with Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses to an Instagram Story, Instagram's camera engine detects the hardware metadata and presents an exclusive circular Spin View button in the editing tray.

Tapping this icon activates Instagram's client-side neural depth estimation model. The model reconstructs a 2.5D depth field from the 2D image, allowing viewers to tilt their smartphones or swipe horizontally across the screen to "look around" foreground subjects with realistic motion parallax.

The Three Detection Triggers

Instagram activates this feature only when all three requirements are met simultaneously:
1. The image is exactly 3024 × 4032 pixels (3:4 portrait aspect ratio).
2. The JPEG APP1 EXIF segment contains Make = "Meta AI" and Model = "Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses 2".
3. ColorSpace is flagged as standard sRGB (0xA001 = 1).

Step-by-Step: Posting Converted Photos to Instagram Story

  1. Reformat the Image with rayBEN: Open the rayBEN Converter, select or drop any photo from your computer or phone, and click Download High-Res JPEG or Share Image.
  2. Save to Camera Roll: On iPhone or Android, ensure the converted JPEG is saved directly in your native Photos or Gallery app. (Avoid forwarding the photo through messaging services like WhatsApp, which compress and strip EXIF data).
  3. Launch Instagram Story: Open Instagram, swipe right from your main feed to enter the Story camera, and tap the gallery thumbnail in the lower-left corner.
  4. Select Your Formatted Photo: Select the newly converted 3:4 portrait photo.
  5. Tap the Circular Spin Icon: Look at the top toolbar (next to stickers, text, and music). You will see a circular badge resembling camera glasses or a rotating 3D sphere. Tap it to toggle the Spin View effect on!
  6. Preview & Share: Tilt your phone back and forth to verify that the 3D parallax depth mesh reacts to motion. Add stickers or captions as desired, then publish your Story.

Why Regular Smartphone Photos Don't Trigger the Effect

Even if your smartphone camera has high resolution (such as 48MP or 200MP), its native aspect ratios are typically 4:3 or 16:9, and its EXIF tags identify the device as an Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or Google Pixel. Without the specific Meta AI hardware signature, Instagram treats the upload as a standard static image.
