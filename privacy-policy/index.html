Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 3, 2026. How we protect your personal privacy and handle information.

Compliance: GDPR, CCPA & Google AdSense • Last Updated: March 2026

1. Zero Image Upload Guarantee

At rayBEN ("we", "us", or "our"), our foremost design principle is privacy by design. When you select, capture, or drag-and-drop a photo into our web application:

  • All image processing is 100% on-device: Decoding, aspect ratio cropping, canvas rasterization, and EXIF segment injection take place entirely inside your device's web browser memory.
  • Zero cloud transmission: Your photos are NEVER transmitted over the internet to our servers or any third-party computing infrastructure.
  • Zero image storage: Because photos remain client-side, we have no database, gallery, or cache of user-submitted images.

2. Automatic Location & GPS Stripping

Original photos taken with modern smartphones often contain precise GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) in their EXIF metadata. During the formatting process, rayBEN systematically clears and removes the GPS IFD table from your output file, preventing accidental location disclosure when you share on social media.

3. Analytics & Usage Metrics

To maintain service stability, optimize user experience, and monitor platform errors, we may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4). Google Analytics collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable technical information such as:

  • Browser type, operating system, and general device category (mobile/desktop).
  • Approximate geographical region (country and city level).
  • Session metrics, conversion initiation counts, and pageview statistics.

Google Analytics does NOT have access to the contents, filenames, or images processed in your browser. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense

We may display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund web hosting and maintenance. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

rayBEN does not set persistent tracking cookies for core functionality. We only utilize temporary browser memory (RAM) and standard session objects (such as temporary Blob URLs) while you are actively viewing or downloading your formatted photo.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, documentation, or social platforms (such as Instagram). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or terms of third-party services.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our client-side architecture, please visit our Contact Page or write to us at privacy@rayben.zplaybox.in.
