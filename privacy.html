Zero Server Telemetry

Privacy Policy & Architecture

Meta Spin Studio was engineered around the principle of strict zero-trust data sovereignty. Your personal memories and images never leave your device.

1. Pure Browser-Side Execution

Every image transformation, resizing calculation, HTML5 Canvas render, and EXIF modification occurs purely within your web browser's memory using JavaScript. No files, binary data, or image blobs are transmitted across any network or cloud server.

2. EXIF & Geolocation Stripping

When you export a prepared 3:4 portrait, the application zeroes out sensitive GPS latitude, longitude, and altitude tags from the binary JPEG buffer. Hardware serial numbers and device identifiers are stripped by default.

3. Direct Instagram Sharing

When you tap "Share to Direct Instagram Story", the photo uses native device sharing (navigator.share) and standard deep-linking (instagram-stories://share). No third-party OAuth permissions or Meta tokens are required or collected.
